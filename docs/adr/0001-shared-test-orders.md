# Friends' orders are visible to the order manager

The first version of 拾刻咖啡 is a private test shared with 5–10 invited friends; customer and management interfaces use Simplified Chinese. The existing WeChat mini program account is a personal account. The mini program and separate responsive web management page share CloudBase for order data; the management page is optimized for desktop viewing while remaining usable on phones. Development starts with local mock data for the menu, cart, and page flows, then integrates WeChat sign-in and CloudBase. Begin with a personal trial environment; verify current pricing and obtain explicit approval before enabling any paid service. Customers must sign in with WeChat before ordering, confirm a pickup name on first use (which may use their WeChat nickname), and may view only their own orders. The order manager uses a single administrator account, manually provisioned by the developer, with no public registration; credentials must be stored securely and never committed to source control. The three fictional coffee items are Americano (Medium ¥18), Latte (Medium ¥22), and Mocha (Medium ¥24); Large adds ¥4. Customers choose sweetness (no sugar, half sugar, or full sugar), temperature (iced or hot), and milk (regular or oat); these customizations are the same across items and do not change the price. A cart can contain multiple configured drinks; customers may set quantity for drinks with identical item, size, and customization choices. Customers choose between Yunshan Shop and Hupan Shop, two fictional shops with the same 09:00–21:00 business hours, and schedule pickup on the same day at future whole-hour slots from 10:00 through 21:00 inclusive, with no fixed lead time. Slots have no capacity limit in the first version. Checkout directly creates a simulated order without any payment step. After submission, an order moves through Pending, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, and Picked Up; customers can view its current status but cannot change or cancel it. In the management page, orders are sorted by pickup time, can be filtered by shop and status, and can only be advanced one status at a time. No coupons, loyalty points, stored-value top-ups, or membership tiers are included. The mini program remains a fictional-brand learning project without real payment or public launch.
